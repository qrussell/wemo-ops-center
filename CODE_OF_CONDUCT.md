# Code of Conduct

Wemo Ops Center is a small project run by one volunteer, with people of every skill level who are trying to keep their devices working. We want issues, pull requests and Discussions to be a friendly place to ask, help and share.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

* Being kind and patient, especially with people new to networking, Linux or smart homes
* Respecting different opinions, setups and favourite hubs
* Giving and accepting constructive feedback
* Owning up to mistakes and learning from them
* Focusing on what is best for the people using the project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

* Insults, put-downs, trolling, or personal or political attacks
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address, IP address or network details, without their permission
* Spam, advertising, or posting malware or links to it
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, Discussions, the wiki and the GitHub and GitLab repositories) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, Quentin Russell, by using **Report content** on the comment or post on GitHub, or by contacting him through his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/qrussell). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, depending on how serious and repeated it is.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report them as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
